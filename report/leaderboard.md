# Leaderboard: Linked-Re-DocRED relation extraction

Generated by `scripts/evaluate.py` from `leaderboard.csv`. Scores in %. See `docs/evaluation.md`, "Reading the leaderboard".

- **Key metrics (rank on these):** F1, then Ign F1.
- **Diagnosis:** P and R; Detection F1 (is the pair related?); Typing F1 (right relation, on detected related pairs); Macro F1 (every relation weighs the same, so rare ones count).
- **Graph F1:** the same facts merged into one corpus graph (entities merged on their Wikidata item).
- With several seeds, a score reads mean ± standard deviation. The 95% interval of F1 comes from resampling documents (bootstrap): two methods whose intervals overlap may not really differ, check with `scripts/compare_runs.py`.
- Choices are made on `dev`. `test` is reported once per method, with frozen settings: until then its rows are marked *provisional*.
