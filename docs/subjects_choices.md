#### News scraping

The main goal is to create a news scraper, that takes as input a list of news paper websites starts collecting the appearing articles and saves their url, title, full-text in a database continuously as long as it is not stopped.

Hamborg, F., Meuschke, N., Breitinger, C. & Gipp, B. (2017). news-please : A generic news crawler and extractor, In 15th International Symposium of Information Science (ISI 2017).
